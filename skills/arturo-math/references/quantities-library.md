# Quantities: function selection

Use for dimensional quantities, units, conversion, custom units, and physical constants. A backtick introduces a unit (see the examples below). Keep the quantity until unit validation is complete; `scalar` removes units. Check dimensions and precision rather than treating unlike quantities as plain numbers. Currency examples may depend on external data and are not part of offline checks.

For signatures, ask the installed runtime with `inspect info.get 'acceleration?`. Read [runtime inspection](runtime.md) before interpreting argument types and attributes.

Examples are independent fragments; names such as `value`, `items`, `settings`, `client`, `server`, `db`, and `childPid` denote inputs or resources prepared by the surrounding program. They are not implicit globals. Keep inputs bounded and inspect literal mutation separately from returned values.
