## Evidence record

Record each supported verification dimension independently: `documented`, `metadata-inspected`, `executed`, `results-asserted`, or `edge-cases-tested`. Include scope, review date, evidence paths, result, and limitations. For runtime verification include the exact command, interpreter version, and platform. State missing historical commands explicitly rather than inventing reproducibility. A successful exit does not establish expected output correctness.

For adapted material identify the upstream source and revision, original author, applicable license, and changes made. Retain local license texts and third-party notices. Automated checks validate required attribution fields and local links; code owners review their accuracy and sufficiency.
